Emergent Lights

Birds × slime mold × fireflies: a few simple rules, growing complex order
How in step the flashing is
What are the rules? (plain English)

Every dot of light is an individual. It can only see a small circle of neighbors around it. There is no leader, and no one knows what the whole picture looks like. At every moment, each dot does just a few small things:

① Personal space: if a neighbor gets too close, step aside a little.

② Go with the flow: notice roughly which way your neighbors are heading, and turn a little that way too.

③ Stay together: drift a little toward where your neighbors are gathered.

④ Follow the scent: everywhere a dot goes, it leaves a faint scent that slowly fades. It leans toward whichever side ahead smells stronger. The more dots walk a path, the clearer the path gets, and the more dots follow it.

⑤ Catch the rhythm: each dot has a little inner clock. When the clock runs out, the dot flashes and starts counting again. Every clock runs at a slightly different pace. When a dot sees a neighbor flash, its own clock gets nudged forward a bit.

What happens? Rules ①②③ turn scattered dots into flocks that swirl, split and merge. Rule ④ carves glowing "rivers," the way slime mold finds paths. Rule ⑤ turns random twinkling into waves of light that flash together. None of these patterns is written into the program. They emerge.

Try it: drag a slider all the way left to switch that rule off, and watch the order fall apart or change shape.
